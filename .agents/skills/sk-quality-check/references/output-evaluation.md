# Output Evaluation

Use this reference to evaluate outputs against a repository-defined or
task-supplied oracle, rubric, sample set, and tolerance.

## Before Evaluation

- Identify the authority for every criterion and threshold.
- Confirm the complete required sample set and expected output identity.
- Record evaluator inputs, rubric revision, environment, and limitations.
- Do not invent missing criteria or silently reinterpret ambiguous thresholds.

## Evidence

For each criterion, report `passed`, `failed`, `blocked`, or `not-evaluated`,
with the observed evidence and affected output. Preserve failed, skipped, and
inconclusive cases. A subjective observation must be labeled as such and tied
to the supplied rubric.

Report `blocked` when the oracle, rubric, required samples, or material context
is missing or internally inconsistent.
